How it works

Four steps, all local.

Structure-aware chunkingThe official PDFs are split into 1,743 passages that keep document, section (SI 3, ITC-BT-25, Article 4…), sub-section and page. Running headers, tables of contents and page numbers are removed.
Hybrid searchBM25 finds literal figures and codes (“ITC-BT-25”, “0,90 m”); bge-m3 embeddings find the meaning even when the question uses other words.
Answer with citationqwen2.5:7b answers only from the 6 retrieved passages, cites them by number and, if the answer is not there, says “No lo encuentro en los documentos indexados” (I can't find it).
Honest evaluationEvery answer is checked against the correct figure and the cited passage. Failures are shown with their cause.

What fails, and what I'd do in a real project

The errors follow a clear pattern.

Flattened tablesExtracting the PDF pulls some table columns apart; this is behind 3 of the 9 misses (door width, structural fire resistance, power that requires a design project). Fix: extract tables as tables and index them row by row.
Neighbouring cases in the same paragraphIn 2 errors the model takes the figure from a nearby exception (evacuation route, ramp slope). Fix: a larger model or a second step that checks the figure against the cited passage.
Citation always on screenEvery answer shows the passage it comes from, so the technician can check it in seconds. With regulations, the assistant helps you search; it doesn't replace reading.
